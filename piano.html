a bit about me. . .

As a music educator turned WordPress Web Designer, I use structured creativity when building intuitive, user-friendly websites. My experience in teaching and liturgical music has shaped my skills in communication and content organization, enabling me to design digital spaces that are both engaging and easy to navigate.

I am passionate about crafting responsive web designs that adapt seamlessly across devices. Comfortable in HTML, CSS, Javascript, and WordPress, I specialize in creating websites that don't just look good - they work effortlessly for diverse users.

Armed with a B.A. in Music Education from Seattle Pacific University, a Full-Stack Web Development certificate from CareerFoundry,
and a deep enthusiasm for all things WordPress, I am excited to collaborate on new and meaningful projects. Whether arranging sacred music for liturgies or arranging elements on a webpage, my approach is mission-driven and intentional.

I am currently working as a freelance Web Designer with Catholics for Hire!
